# Finance REM-Card Capture and Reconciliation

Open [`case-study.pdf`](case-study.pdf) for the visual explanation.

This case study explains how handwritten physical REM cards and related finance records were converted into structured tables, validated, reconciled, and reviewed for variances. The workflow covers REM-card number, account number, balances, cash issued, chip payments, cash payments, vouchers in/out, VAT in/out, free bets, match play logs, and exception reporting. Casino and client details are intentionally anonymized.
